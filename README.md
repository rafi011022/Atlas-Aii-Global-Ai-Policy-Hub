## 📖 About Atlas Aii

**Atlas Aii Global AI Policy Hub** develops independent, research-based AI governance frameworks for nations in the Global South — countries that are often underrepresented in mainstream AI policy discourse.

Our policies are:
- 📚 Aligned with global standards: EU AI Act, UNESCO Recommendation on AI, OECD AI Principles, ITU AI for Good, G20 AI Principles
- 🏛️ Locally contextualized for each nation's political, economic, and social realities
- 🔓 Freely available under Creative Commons license
- 🌍 Covering 97 nations across Africa, South Asia, Southeast Asia, MENA, and Latin America
